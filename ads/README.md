# Google Ads launch package

`google-ads-launch.json` is the reviewed build specification for a paused Alberta Search launch.

Before enabling either campaign:

1. Use a Canadian Google Ads account with CAD billing and the Edmonton time zone. Do not reuse the cancelled INR account.
2. Keep both campaigns paused until the production form accepts a controlled test enquiry and the Google Ads conversion is recorded exactly once.
3. Confirm the account uses Alberta presence targeting, Google Search only, exact and phrase keywords, and the shared negative list.
4. Keep AI Max, text customization, Final URL Expansion, Display expansion, and Search Partners off for the initial test.
5. Validate every ad and asset against its landing page before policy submission.
6. Set the campaign goal to the successful enquiry conversion. Form starts and CTA clicks are observation events only.
7. Review search terms twice weekly and record qualified-lead outcomes outside the website.

The C$60/C$40 daily split is a launch placeholder, not authorization to spend. Billing and campaign activation require explicit owner approval.
